## 3.3 Chapter summary (EMA32)

Presentation: 2F85

- The general term is expressed as the \(n^{\text{th}}\) term and is written as \({T}_{n}\).
- We define the common difference \(d\) of a sequence as the difference between any two successive
                    terms, where \(d=T_{n}-T(n-1)\)
- We can work out a general formula for each number pattern and use it to determine any term in the
                    pattern.
