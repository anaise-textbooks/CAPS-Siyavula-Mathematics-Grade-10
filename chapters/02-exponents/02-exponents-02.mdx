## 2.3 Rational exponents (EMAV)

We can also apply the exponent laws to expressions with rational exponents.

According to CAPS, the rational exponent law is introduced in Grade 11 but you may choose to introduce learners
        to the rational exponent law \(a^{\frac{m}{n}}=\sqrt[n]{a^{m}}\) at this stage.

<Callout type="tip" title="Worked example 6: Simplifying rational exponents">
**Question:**
Simplify:

        \[2{x}^{\frac{1}{2}}\times 4{x}^{-\frac{1}{2}}\]

\begin{align*}
      2{x}^{\frac{1}{2}} \times 4{x}^{-\frac{1}{2}} & = 8{x}^{\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2}}\\
      & = 8{x}^{0} \\
      & = 8\left(1\right) \\
      & = 8
      \end{align*}
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Worked example 7: Simplifying rational exponents">
**Question:**
Simplify:

        \[{\left(\text{0,008}\right)}^{\frac{1}{3}}\]

**Write as a fraction and simplify:**
\begin{align*}
        {\left(\text{0,008}\right)}^{\frac{1}{3}} & = {\left(\frac{8}{\text{1 000}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{3}} \\
        & = {\left(\frac{1}{125}\right)}^{\frac{1}{3}} \\
        & = {\left(\frac{1}{5^{3}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{3}} \\
        & = \frac{{1}^{\frac{1}{3}}}{5^{\left(3 \cdot \frac{1}{3}\right)}} \\
        & = \frac{1}{5}
        \end{align*}
</Callout>

### Textbook Exercise 2.2

Simplify without using a calculator:

**Problem:**
\(t^{\frac{1}{4}} \times 3t^{\frac{7}{4}}\)

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
            t^{\frac{1}{4}} \times 3t^{\frac{7}{4}} & = 3t^{\frac{1}{4} + \frac{7}{4}} \\
            & = 3t^{\frac{8}{4}} \\
            & = 3t^{2}
            \end{align*}

**Problem:**
\(\dfrac{16x^{2}}{\left(4x^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}}\)

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
            \frac{16x^{2}}{\left(4x^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}} & =
            \frac{4^{2}x^{2}}{4^{\frac{1}{2}}x^{(2)\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)}} \\
            & = \frac{4^{2}x^{2}}{4^{\frac{1}{2}}x} \\
            & = 4^{2 - \frac{1}{2}} \cdot x^{2 - 1} \\
            & = \left(2^{2}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}x \\
            & = 2^{3}x \\
            & = 8x
            \end{align*}

**Problem:**
\(\left(\text{0,25}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\)

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
            \left(\text{0,25}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} & = \left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} \\
            & = \left(\dfrac{1}{2^{2}}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} \\
            & = \left(2^{-2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} \\
            & = 2^{-1} \\
            & = \dfrac{1}{2}
            \end{align*}

**Problem:**
\(\left(27\right)^{-\frac{1}{3}}\)

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
            \left(27\right)^{-\frac{1}{3}} & = \left(3^{3}\right)^{-\frac{1}{3}} \\
            & = 3^{-1} \\
            & = \dfrac{1}{3}
            \end{align*}

**Problem:**
\(\left(3p^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} \times \left(3p^{4}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\)

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
            \left(3p^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} \times \left(3p^{4}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} & = 3^{\frac{1}{2}}p \times
            3^{\frac{1}{2}}p^{2} \\
            & = 3^{\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2}} \times p^{1 + 2} \\
            & = 3p^{3}
            \end{align*}

**Problem:**
\(\text{12} {\left( a^\text{4}b^\text{8} \right)}^ {\frac{\text{1}}{\text{2}}} \times {\left(
              \text{512}a^\text{3}b^\text{3} \right)}^ {\frac{\text{1}}{\text{3}}}\)

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
              \text{12} {\left( a^\text{4}b^\text{8} \right)}^ {\frac{\text{1}}{\text{2}}} \times {\left(
              \text{512}a^\text{3}b^\text{3} \right)}^ {\frac{\text{1}}{\text{3}}}
              &= \text{12} a^{\frac{\text{4}}{\text{2}}}b^ {\frac{\text{8}}{\text{2}}} \times
              (\text{512})^{\frac{\text{1}}{\text{3}}}a^{\frac{\text{3}}{\text{3}}}b^{\frac{\text{3}}{\text{3}}} \\
              &= \text{12} a^{\text{2}}b^{\text{4}} \times \left( \text{8}^{\text{3}} \right)
              ^{\frac{\text{1}}{\text{3}}}a^{\text{1}}b^{\text{1}} \\
              &= \text{12} a^{\text{2}}b^{\text{4}} \times \text{8}a^{\text{1}}b^{\text{1}} \\
              &= \text{96} a^{\text{3}} b^{\text{5}}
              \end{align*}

**Problem:**
\(\left((-2)^4a^6b^2\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\)

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
            \left((-2)^4a^6b^2\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} & = (-2)^2(a^3b) \\
            & = 4a^3b
            \end{align*}

**Problem:**
\(\left(a^{-2}b^6\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\)

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
            \left(a^{-2}b^6\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} & = a^{-1}b^3 \\
            & = \frac{b^{3}}{a}
            \end{align*}

**Problem:**
\(\left(16x^{12}b^6\right)^{\frac{1}{3}}\)

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
            \left(16x^{12}b^6\right)^{\frac{1}{3}} & = \left((8 \times 2) x^{12}b^{6}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}} \\
            & = 2\cdot 2^{\frac{1}{3}}a^{4}b^{2}
            \end{align*}
